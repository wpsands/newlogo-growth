# Pipeline audit: Outreach

2026-11-15 · outside-in, public data only · prepared with newlogo-growth v0.2.0 · by Page Sands

Coverage: Firecrawl scrape and search, raw page fetches, the public GTM container and Marketo form definitions, the Lever postings API, and the Google Ads Transparency Center (through a Firecrawl data provider, ad text read by OCR). Semrush failed ("API UNITS BALANCE IS ZERO") and Ahrefs failed ("Insufficient plan"), so there is no keyword, ranking or paid-spend data; search positions come from single web-search runs and are `directional`. AI answers come from Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity queried directly through OpenRouter with each provider's own web search, five buyer questions, three runs each, on 2026-11-17. An earlier search-engine read of surface 6 was replaced by that probe. No enrichment was run, so customer sizes and regions are `inferred` from general knowledge. The LinkedIn company page was read through a data-provider mirror dated 2026-11-12, not the live page.

This is a public example. It uses public sources only and has not been reviewed by Outreach. It contains no internal performance data.

## Summary

Outreach has moved its story from sales engagement to an agentic AI platform for revenue teams, and its own site, press and LinkedIn tell it. The biggest constraint on new-logo pipeline sits upstream: no public surface names a segment or trigger narrow enough to build an account list, so the clearest switching moment in the category (Salesloft and Clari merged in December 2025 and moved to one brand in September 2026) reaches buyers only as a comparison page that ends in a 12-field demo form. Elsewhere, buyers still read "sales engagement platform" or "Sales Execution Platform" on all five third-party surfaces checked.

Top priorities:
1. Name a list-able segment and trigger, starting with accounts running Salesloft and Clari.
2. Add an offer between content and the demo form, starting with the product tour Outreach already has.
3. Carry the current story to third-party surfaces, and use third-party proof with in-market accounts.
4. Give security and procurement reviewers a path that does not wait for an account executive.

## Profile

Stage: enterprise (`inferred`). Founded 2014, $489M raised, $4.4B valuation after its Series G (outreach.ai/company/about); Databricks, SAP, Siemens and Verizon in press boilerplate; a Leader for Gartner (Revenue Action Orchestration, 2026-10-15), Forrester (2026-09-24) and IDC (2026-09-21) per the newsroom; offices in Seattle, Prague, Hyderabad and Atlanta; a three-track partner program. Commercial AE (10 to 1,000 employees) and Enterprise AE (5,000 to 10,000) roles show it sells across segments.

Motion: sales-led (`observed`). No public prices, trial or self-serve purchase; every primary call to action is "Get a demo" or "Request pricing". AWS Marketplace offers a procurement route that is still sales-assisted.

Stated ICP: "revenue teams" adopting AI, with enterprise-weighted proof (Siemens, Avis Budget Group, SAP, Verizon) and pricing copy that steers CROs of "enterprise revenue teams" to Amplify Pro (`observed`).
Observed ICP: B2B software, security, data and payments companies with SDR and AE teams, mostly 1,000+ employees in the named-customer set, on top of a public reviewer base that is mostly mid-market software (`inferred`; sizes not enriched).

## Plays

Results on counts of opportunities in the tens are `directional`. Compare with matched holdouts; do not claim significance.

### Play 1. Merger-window consolidation review
Acts on: L1-1, L1-4, L4-1, L4-3 · Priority: 1
Segment: US and EMEA B2B companies, 200 to 5,000 employees, running Salesloft, Clari or both on Salesforce · Trigger: the Salesloft and Clari merger (completed December 2025, one brand September 2026), visible in job posts and at renewal
Personas: RevOps lead (feels the problem) · CRO or VP Sales (signs) · IT or security reviewer (blocks)

Step 1. RevOps lead gets a written stack review (overlapping tools, what the merger changes) and the self-guided tour by email. Owner AE.
  If they reply or open the tour, then a 30-minute session with a solutions consultant, owner AE, stage first meeting.
  If no response in 7 days, then the review goes to the CRO, owner AE.
Step 2. CRO gets a consolidation business case (tools retired, seats, AI credit tier) and the Freshworks story. Owner AE. If security comes up, the priority 4 pack, owner AE.
  If they agree, then a 30-day pilot on one team with a written success criterion, owner solutions consultant, stage opportunity.
Exit. Opportunity, or disqualified: no renewal inside 12 months (set a reminder 120 days before it), or too few seats.
Stops when. Three touches per persona, or 30 days.

Why it should work: 67% of B2B buyers prefer a rep-free experience [E-03] and 73% of B2B buyers avoid suppliers who send irrelevant outreach [E-09]. No third-party evidence found that a vendor merger raises switching rates.
Test: list accounts beat a matched holdout on opportunities within 10 weeks.
Metric: opportunities per 100 accounts worked · Leading indicator: RevOps replies and tour opens, weeks 2 and 3
Expected lift: low, no difference; mid, more first meetings; high, higher opportunity rate; plan on low · Prerequisites: account list with tool data, matched holdout, opportunity source recorded at creation
Owner: sales leadership with revenue operations · Review: week 10 · Kill rule: no more first meetings than holdout by week 6 · Scale rule: opportunity rate above holdout at two reviews; extend to Gong-only accounts

### Play 2. Tour first, readout second
Acts on: L4-1, L4-2, L4-4, L2-4 · Priority: 2
Segment: B2B software and IT services companies, 51 to 1,000 employees, on Salesforce or HubSpot, outside Play 1's list · Trigger: someone completes a calculator or the AI maturity quiz, attends the AI maturity webinar, or the account shows an intent surge
Personas: sales development or operations manager (feels the problem) · VP Sales or CRO (signs)

Step 1. Manager gets the tour and a one-page readout against Outreach Insights Group benchmarks within one business day. Owner marketing operations.
  If they reply or book, then a 30-minute readout with a solutions consultant, owner Commercial AE, stage first meeting.
  If no response in 5 days, then one same-size customer story (WithMe or Rootly), owner marketing operations.
Step 2. VP Sales gets the readout summary and a cost model at the right AI credit tier. Owner AE.
  If they agree, then a scoped pilot proposal, owner AE, stage opportunity; if they ask for price first, a written price range.
Exit. Opportunity, or disqualified for too few seats.
Stops when. Two touches per persona, or 21 days.

Why it should work: two out of three software buyers prefer to engage vendor salespeople only in the later stages of their buying journey [E-16, vendor-own]; 40% of technology buyers name "having to contact sales for a demo or free trial" among the top three things that make them less likely to buy [E-15, vendor-own, 2022].
Test: triggered accounts given the tour and readout beat the demo-form group on first meetings within 8 weeks.
Metric: first meetings per 100 triggered accounts, then opportunities · Leading indicator: tour completions and readout bookings, weeks 2 and 3
Expected lift: low, same first meetings, fewer form fields; mid, more first meetings; high, more meetings and opportunities; plan on low · Prerequisites: tool completions tied to the account; demo conversion counted once (L6-1)
Owner: marketing operations with the Commercial sales team · Review: week 8 · Kill rule: no more first meetings than the demo-form group by week 6 · Scale rule: more first meetings, equal or better meeting-to-opportunity rate at week 8

### Play 3. Third-party proof for accounts already comparing
Acts on: L3-1, L3-2, L3-3, L3-4, L5-1 · Priority: 3
Segment: Play 1 or Play 2 accounts · Trigger: a visit to Outreach comparison pages, G2 compare pages or the Gartner Peer Insights market in the last 14 days
Personas: RevOps or sales operations lead (evaluates) · CRO (signs) · procurement (blocks on a formal shortlist)

Step 1. Evaluator gets a proof pack: the 2026 Gartner, Forrester and IDC placements, Peer Insights and G2 reviews from their size band, and a one-platform versus merging-products comparison. Owner AE.
  If they reply, then a 30-minute session with a customer reference or solutions consultant, owner AE, stage first meeting.
  If no response in 7 days, then the CRO gets a two-line summary with the pack, owner AE.
Step 2. CRO gets a same-industry reference call. Owner AE. If procurement appears, the priority 4 pack, owner AE.
  If they agree, then a scoped evaluation plan, owner AE, stage opportunity.
Exit. Opportunity, or disqualified because the account signed elsewhere.
Stops when. Three touches per persona, or 30 days.

Why it should work: B2B buyers value third-party interactions 1.4 times more than digital supplier interactions [E-17]; software buyers name AI chatbots as the top shortlist influence [E-19, vendor-own]; 69% of B2B buyers report inconsistencies between a supplier's website and its sellers [E-26].
Test: after the fixes, researching accounts sent the pack beat a comparison group on first meetings within 12 weeks.
Metric: first meetings from research-signal accounts · Leading indicator: Outreach named in assistant answers to five buyer questions (3 runs each, monthly)
Expected lift: low, surfaces updated, meetings unchanged; mid, answer inclusion rises; high, both rise; plan on low · Prerequisites: in weeks 1 to 4, product marketing updates Gartner Peer Insights, AWS Marketplace, the marketplace.outreach.io tile, Crunchbase and the request-demo title and meta, retires older ads and picks one customer count; a direct AI-assistant baseline
Owner: product marketing with sales leadership · Review: week 12 · Kill rule: no inclusion change by week 8 and no first-meeting difference · Scale rule: both up at week 12

**Priority 4, committee pack.** Segment: demo or pricing requests in the 751 to 4,999 and 5,000+ bands · First offer: a forwardable security and procurement pack (SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, 27701 and 42001, HIPAA, CSA STAR; production-domain sub-processors; AI data-use terms; order-form outline; AI credit model) · Metric: days from first meeting to security approval. Evidence: buying groups of five to 16 people across as many as four functions [E-10]; buyers who experience buying-group relevance are three times more likely to report a high-quality deal [E-07].
