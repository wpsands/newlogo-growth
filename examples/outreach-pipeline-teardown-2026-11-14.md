# Outreach: where new-logo pipeline is leaking

November 14, 2026 · outside-in, public data only · by Page Sands, using newlogo-growth

Outreach has rebuilt its story around agents. The homepage now reads "Agentic AI Platform for Revenue Teams", the domain moved to outreach.ai, and Gartner named it a Leader in Revenue Action Orchestration in October. Buyers who start their search somewhere other than outreach.ai still meet the old company. None of the six third-party surfaces I checked uses the new story. Search answers describe Outreach as a sales engagement platform and leave it out when a buyer asks for an AI revenue platform or an AI SDR tool.

## What I found

1. The new story stops at Outreach's own site. 5 of 10 Outreach-owned surfaces carry the agentic story. 0 of 6 third-party surfaces do. Gartner Peer Insights still describes "Outreach Sales Execution Platform", and Crunchbase calls it "a sales engagement platform" with outreach.io as its site. [gartner.com/reviews/product/outreach-agentic-ai-platform, crunchbase.com/organization/outreach]
2. Outreach is missing from the answers its new category depends on. Search answers for "best AI revenue platform for mid-market sales teams" and "best AI SDR tools" listed HubSpot, Salesforce, ZoomInfo, Salesloft and Clari, and did not include Outreach. [search-engine answers, US, 2026-11-14, directional]
3. The biggest switching trigger in the category has only a demo behind it. Salesloft and Clari merged in December 2025 and moved to one brand in September 2026. Outreach's comparison page says so and argues for consolidation, and its only call to action, repeated six times, is "Request a demo", which opens a form with 12 required fields. [outreach.ai/salesloft-vs-outreach, outreach.ai/request-demo]

Story sync is 5 of 16 buyer-facing surfaces. Owned surfaces are 5 of 10 and third-party surfaces 0 of 6.

## What I would run in the first 90 days

| # | Experiment | Pipeline metric | Review |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Consolidation assessment for Salesloft and Clari accounts. Build a named list from tech-stack data and offer RevOps leaders a two-week readout of their engagement and forecasting stack, admin load and a migration plan. | Opportunities created from list accounts vs a matched holdout | Week 10 |
| 2 | Third-party story sync. Update the Gartner Peer Insights description, G2 category placement and Crunchbase, and pitch the analysts and listicle authors behind AI SDR and revenue-platform roundups. Track inclusion across 20 buyer prompts each month. | First meetings from ICP accounts | Week 12 |
| 3 | A middle offer. Turn the existing pipeline and ROI calculators into a short assessment with a human readout, behind a 4-field form, and compare it with the demo form. | Opportunities per form submission | Week 8 |

Each experiment has a kill rule. The consolidation assessment stops if list accounts book no more first meetings than the holdout by week 5.

To confirm this I would need win/loss notes on deals lost to Salesloft or Gong, pipeline by source with its definitions, and the share of demo requests that become opportunities by seat-count band.
