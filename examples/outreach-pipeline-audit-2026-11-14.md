# Pipeline audit: Outreach

November 14, 2026 · outside-in, public data only · prepared with newlogo-growth v0.1.0

This is a sample output. It uses public sources only and has not been reviewed by Outreach. Findings marked `directional` rest on search-engine answers, because the run could not query ChatGPT, Perplexity or Gemini directly.

## Summary

Outreach has repositioned from sales engagement to an agentic AI platform for revenue teams, and its own site, press and LinkedIn tell that story. The constraint on new-logo pipeline is that buyers mostly meet Outreach elsewhere first, and no third-party surface checked carries the new story. The second constraint is the offer. A live switching trigger (the Salesloft and Clari merger) and a library of calculators and quizzes all lead to one 12-field demo form, with nothing in between.

Top priorities:
1. Define an ICP that can produce a named-account list, starting with Salesloft and Clari accounts.
2. Carry the agentic story to third-party surfaces and AI answers.
3. Add a middle offer between content and demo, starting with a consolidation assessment.

## Profile

Stage: enterprise. Private, founded 2014, last priced round a $200M Series G in June 2021 at a valuation above $4.4B (PR Newswire). Workforce reductions in 2023 and 2024 (GeekWire). Motion: sales-led. No self-serve purchase and no public prices; every call to action is a demo or pricing request.

Stated ICP: large enterprises ("larger deployments", /enterprise; Forrester TEI composite of $9B revenue and 20,000 employees). Observed ICP: B2B software, security and tech-enabled services companies with large SDR and AE teams, plus a visible set of mid-market and smaller customers in the story library (WithMe, Rootly, Traction Complete, Omniplex Learning).

## Findings

### ICP
- **L1-1** · The stated ICP is large enterprise (/enterprise; TEI composite of $9B revenue and 20,000 employees), while the story library includes several mid-market and smaller customers and the pricing FAQ points "mid-sized teams" to the Plus tier. There are no segment or industry pages in the navigation. Evidence: outreach.ai/enterprise; outreach.ai/resources/stories; outreach.ai/pricing. Observed. P2. Effect: accounts reached.
- **L1-2** · Outreach's comparison page states that Salesloft and Clari "merged in December 2025 and consolidated under one brand in September 2026"; clari.com now redirects to salesloft.com. This is a datable trigger for a named-account list. Evidence: outreach.ai/salesloft-vs-outreach; HTTP 301 from clari.com, 2026-11-14. Observed. Opportunity.

### Audiences
- **L2-1** · Role pages exist for RevOps, sales leaders, AEs, sales managers, sales development and customer success; the AE and sales manager pages share the same lead sentence. Evidence: outreach.ai homepage navigation. Observed. P3.
- **L2-2** · No page addresses finance, IT and security, or procurement, although pricing combines seats with consumption credits. Trust-center access goes through an account executive. Evidence: outreach.ai/pricing; outreach.ai/platform/trust. Observed. P2. Effect: win rate, cycle length.

### Messaging
- **L3-1** · Story sync is 5 of 16 surfaces: owned 5 of 10, third-party 0 of 6. Evidence: story sync table below. Observed. P1. Root cause: the repositioning has not reached surfaces Outreach does not publish.
- **L3-2** · Gartner Peer Insights lists "Outreach Agentic AI Platform" but describes "Outreach Sales Execution Platform"; Crunchbase describes "a sales engagement platform" and lists outreach.io. Evidence: gartner.com/reviews/product/outreach-agentic-ai-platform; crunchbase.com/organization/outreach. Observed. P2.
- **L3-3** · Search answers for "best AI revenue platform for mid-market sales teams" and "best AI SDR tools" did not include Outreach; answers to "What is Outreach?" led with "sales engagement platform". Evidence: two search engines, one run each, 2026-11-14. Directional. P2.
- **L3-4** · Proof figures differ across pages: forecast prep "44%" (AI Agents, Forecasting) vs "half the time" (Pricing); customer count "5,000+" (ROI calculator) vs "6000" (Apollo comparison). Evidence: page sources 2026-11-14. Observed. P3.

### Offers
- **L4-1** · The Salesloft, Gong and Clari comparison pages offer only "Request a demo". No switching assessment, pilot or migration offer appears on any page checked. Evidence: outreach.ai/salesloft-vs-outreach (6 instances of "Request a demo"), /gong-vs-outreach, /clari-vs-outreach. Observed. P1.
- **L4-2** · The tools hub holds two calculators and six quizzes or checklists; each one's next step is a demo. Evidence: outreach.ai/resources/tools. Observed. P2.
- **L4-3** · The demo form has 12 required fields, including seat count, email provider and current CRM. Evidence: Marketo form 1029 definition. Observed. P2.

### Motion and channels
- **L5-1** · Of 16 open roles, 2 are AE roles and none are SDR, BDR, marketing or partner roles; 6sense, Terminus and Qualified are installed. Pipeline creation appears to rest with AEs, signals and agents. Evidence: api.lever.co/v0/postings/outreach; GTM container GTM-MVB4J5L. Observed; pipeline effect needs data. P3.
- **L5-2** · Of 6 active search ads sampled, 3 use older or generic copy. Evidence: Google Ads Transparency Center, advertiser "Outreach Corporation". Observed, small sample, no spend data. P3.

### Measurement
- **L6-1** · The site runs on outreach.ai while Marketo forms, the app, support, university and marketplace remain on outreach.io subdomains, and the Google Ads linker list does not include them. Evidence: GTM container; page sources. Inferred; impact needs data. P3.

## Parking lot
- Pricing page states "Per user pricing. No platform fees." alongside consumption credits.
- The AI Agents FAQ lists three agents while the page lists seven plus Agent Studio.
- The only ungated product tour sits on the Apollo comparison page, which the comparison hub does not link.
- No HubSpot comparison page, while the demo form lists HubSpot as a CRM option.
