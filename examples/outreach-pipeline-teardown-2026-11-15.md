# Outreach: where pipeline is leaking

2026-11-15 · outside-in, public data only · by Page Sands

Coverage: public pages, page source, ad library and job posts. Semrush failed ("API UNITS BALANCE IS ZERO") and Ahrefs failed ("Insufficient plan"), so search positions are directional. AI answers come from Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity queried directly through OpenRouter with each provider's own web search, five buyer questions, three runs each, on 2026-11-17. No enrichment was run, so customer sizes are inferred. LinkedIn was read through a data-provider mirror dated 2026-11-12.

**The constraint.** The ICP. Outreach describes its buyer as "revenue teams", and no public surface names a segment or trigger narrow enough to build an account list. The clearest switching moment in the category, the Salesloft and Clari merger, reaches buyers only as a comparison page that ends in a 12-field demo form.

**What I found**
1. The site, forms and job posts use three different size schemes, and the proof leads with Siemens and Avis while 59% of G2 reviewers who state a size work at companies with 51 to 1,000 employees (`inferred`: reviews are cumulative and customer sizes were not enriched). [outreach.ai, Marketo form 1029, Lever postings, g2.com/products/outreach/reviews]
2. The new story stops at Outreach's own surfaces. Crunchbase calls Outreach "a sales engagement platform", AWS Marketplace lists "Outreach Sales Execution Platform", and the Gartner Peer Insights description begins "Outreach Sales Execution Platform is a software designed to assist sales teams". The request-demo page title still reads "AI Sales Engagement Platform". [crunchbase.com, aws.amazon.com/marketplace, gartner.com/reviews, outreach.ai/request-demo]
3. Two live ads promise "See the platform for yourself, without even talking to sales", and a self-guided tour exists at demo.outreach.io, but the home, pricing, enterprise and request-demo pages do not link it. Every page leads to a demo or pricing request. [Google Ads Transparency Center, outreach.ai blog, demo.outreach.io]

**Story sync:** 4 of 10 buyer-facing surfaces tell the current story (owned 4 of 5, third-party 0 of 5). Biggest gap: review and marketplace listings (Gartner Peer Insights, AWS Marketplace).

**The play I would run first: merger-window consolidation review**
Segment: B2B companies with 200 to 5,000 employees running Salesloft or Clari on Salesforce · Trigger: the merger (completed December 2025, one brand September 2026) and the account's renewal date · Personas: RevOps lead (feels it) · CRO (signs) · security (blocks)
- RevOps lead gets a written stack review and the product tour from the territory AE. If they reply or open the tour, a 30-minute session with a solutions consultant (first meeting).
- If no response in 7 days, the same review goes to the CRO with a one-paragraph renewal summary.
- If the CRO agrees to a call, a scoped 30-day pilot with a written success criterion (opportunity). If renewal is more than 9 months out, a reminder 120 days before it.
- Stops after three touches per persona or 30 days.
Metric: opportunities per 100 accounts worked. Kill rule: no more first meetings than a matched holdout by week 6.
Why it should work: 67% of B2B buyers prefer a rep-free experience, so the first step gives value before asking for time [E-03, Gartner 2026, analyst]. No third-party evidence found that vendor mergers raise switching.
