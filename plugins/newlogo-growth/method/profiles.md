# Company profile: stage × motion

Profile the company before auditing it. The profile decides which checks carry the most weight and which experiments are allowed. State the profile and the evidence for it at the top of every output. If evidence is thin, give the most likely profile and mark it `inferred`.

## Stage

| Stage | Typical signals | Where pipeline usually breaks | Weight the audit toward |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Seed with early PMF** | Founder-led sales, under ~20 customers, site sells a vision, few case studies | ICP not yet proven; founder is the only motion; no repeatable first meeting | ICP proof from actual customers, one narrow audience, founder-led signal outbound, a credible first offer |
| **Series A** | First sales hires, a named category, early case studies | Stated ICP broader than the customers who actually win; story differs across site, deck and outbound; reps lack signals | ICP narrowing, a named-account list (~300 to 500), contextual signals (hires, funding, incidents), messaging sync, speed to first human response |
| **Series B** | Multiple segments or products, marketing team, paid programs | Motions added before the weakest is fixed; category education costs; measurement that cannot attribute | Segment-level ICP, audience-specific messaging, offer ladder per segment, AI visibility and share of voice against 3 to 5 competitors, deal-source definitions |
| **Mid-market (often PE-backed)** | Several products, acquisitions, agency sprawl, legacy CRM | GTM debt: fragmented story after acquisitions, undefined sourced vs influenced, phantom opportunities, slow follow-up | Story sync across product lines, measurement repair before lift claims, routing and SLAs, consolidating overlapping programs |
| **Enterprise** | Large field team, partner channel, regional teams, often a self-serve store | Teams competing for the same buyer; long cycles hide leaks; self-serve buyers never reach sales | Buying-committee coverage, account-level orchestration, self-serve to enterprise routing, persona-level AI visibility, governance of who owns which segment |

Below seed (no paying customers, no repeatable fit): say plainly that the constraint is product-market fit, not pipeline, and limit output to an ICP hypothesis and a customer-discovery plan.

## Motion

| Motion | Signals | Pipeline questions to answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Sales-led** | "Book a demo" is the main call to action; no public pricing | Does the first offer earn a meeting from the right audience? How fast does a human respond? |
| **Product-led** | Free plan or self-serve trial, public pricing, in-app upgrade | Which signups match the ICP? Is there a product-qualified account definition, and does it route to sales quickly? |
| **Hybrid self-serve + enterprise** | A store or self-serve checkout alongside an enterprise sales team | Are self-serve buyers treated as enterprise and cross-sell signals? Are low-ACV buyers routed away from sales and high-fit ones routed to it? Do the two teams compete for the same accounts or search terms? |

Product-led motions count here only as a source of pipeline. Onboarding and activation design are out of scope unless they block a pipeline play.

## Existing customers

New-logo pipeline is the default focus. For mid-market and enterprise profiles, note expansion and renewal risk where the evidence shows it, but do not build the plan around it.
